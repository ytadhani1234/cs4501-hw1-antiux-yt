Hotel Antiusabilius — HW1 Anti-UX Documentation

Return to booking home

Task

Book the cheapest available room for two adults at Hotel Antiusabilius in Charlottesville, Virginia for November 14–15, 2026 without premium upgrades and complete the reservation using fake guest information.

The site is a deterministic classroom simulation. It collects no payments and sends no data to a server.

Completion Cheatsheet

  1. Begin the reservation.
  2. Choose Charlottesville, VA.
  3. Choose year 2026.
  4. Choose Q4.
  5. Choose November.
  6. Choose Week 2.
  7. Choose November 14.
  8. Choose 2 adults.
  9. Continue to room results.
  10. Notice the pricing rule in the ad-like banner: tax applies to base rate only.
  11. Determine that Room B — Questionable Deluxe — is cheapest.
  12. Click the boring “Apply” button on Room B.
  13. Remember code B-417-K and acknowledge the panel.
  14. Proceed.
  15. Leave all premium upgrades OFF.
  16. Enter fake guest information.
  17. On the review screen enter B-417-K.
  18. Click the RED “Finalize Reservation” button.
  19. Reach “Reservation Complete.”

Room calculations

Room A: $80 + $19 + 12% of $80 = $108.60

Room B: $86 + $8 + 8% of $86 = $100.88

Room C: $75 + $25 + 10% of $75 = $107.50

Correct cheapest room: Room B.
